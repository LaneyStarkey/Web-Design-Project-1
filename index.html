Y2k

An internet aesthetic centered around the style of the late 90's

Where Y2k came from

The term Y2k came from the year 2000 problem, which was the potential for computers to malfunction as a result of the turn of the century.

The style is often used today as a reference to a more nostalgic era, specifically before the fast advancments in technology in the early 2000s.

The Y2k style is often characterized by bright colors as a reference to the '70s, often with chrome elements. Animal print is used as a reference to the 90's, also often in bright colors.
